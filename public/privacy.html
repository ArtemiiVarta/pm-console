PM Console

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

PM Console is a private project-planning tool run by its owner (GitHub: ArtemiiVarta) for people they invite. This page explains what information it uses and why.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics: name, email address and profile picture, received when you choose “Sign in with Google”. They are used only to check that your email is on the access list and to show who is signed in.
  • Project data you enter: projects, schedules, team members, rates and forecasts.
  • Access list: the email addresses and roles (admin or user) that admins add, and who added them.

We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.

Where it is stored

  • Project data and the access list are stored in a private GitHub repository controlled by the owner. Each saved change records the email of the person who made it.
  • Requests pass through a small server on Cloudflare Workers, which checks your sign-in. It does not keep a copy of your data.
  • Your browser keeps a working copy of the projects and your sign-in session (up to 30 days). Logging out removes them from that browser.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell or share your information with anyone outside the people who use this console.
  • Your Google information is not used for any purpose other than signing you in.

Who can see your data

Everyone with access to PM Console can see the projects in it. Admins can also see the access list. GitHub and Cloudflare host the data and process requests on our behalf under their own privacy terms.

Your choices

You can stop using PM Console at any time and revoke its access from your Google account settings. To have your email removed from the access list, or information you entered corrected or deleted, ask an admin of the console or contact the owner through GitHub.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated.
